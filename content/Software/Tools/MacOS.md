# Why I use MacOS

I have daily driven many operating systems before finding one that perfectly meets my day to day needs. I used *Windows* for many years after building my first computer in 2014 and getting a Surface Pro 3 and XPS 15. Windows was great for playing games like Team Fortress 2 and CSGO but I always struggled with getting the terminal to work well. I also encountered many issues with battery drain on my XPS 15 while the lid was closed and I found that my computer would be hot and dead when I pulled it out of my bad.
