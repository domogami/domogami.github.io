# Network Attached Storage (NAS)

A storage device that is connected to your home network and allows stored files to be easily accessed by devices on that network or across the internet.

## My Personal NAS

I personally have a [Synology 720+](https://www.amazon.com/Synology-Bay-DiskStation-DS720-Diskless/dp/B087Z6SNC1) set up at my parents house with 16TB of storage with 8TB of effective storage in [RAID 1](../RAID%201.md) for redundancy. I setup my entire family to have their phone's backup their photos with the [Synology Photos](https://www.synology.com/en-global/dsm/feature/photos) app.

## Reasoning

The biggest reason why I decided to select a Synology NAS is because of their OS support and built in features. I had just gone through an Arch Linux phase after being inspired by [this XMonad video](https://youtu.be/rMoUCnqhYfs?si=88J4VVHKzhS_syKl) and I was very very tried of doing everything myself and having constant versioning issues. I felt like every time I tried to update a package or get two packages to work well together I would go down a rabbit hole of untangling dependencies and I had gotten sick of it. I wanted something that just worked. I looked into [NextCloud](https://nextcloud.com/) and it looked promising but when I found Synology it looked even more hands off. 

My Synology NAS has been easy to manage for the most part and overall unproblematic.
