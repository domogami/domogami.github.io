# What is a Digital Garden?

A digital garden is a collection of thoughts and notes that grows as I continue to learn. Connections form organically as I capture ideas and notes begin to reference each other. Occasionally, I tend to this garden and prune when necessary but there is something beautiful about this messy process I have come to enjoy.
