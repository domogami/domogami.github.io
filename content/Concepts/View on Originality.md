# What if my thoughts are not original?

I am realizing as I create this digital garden that not all of my content will be original and actually as I learn and find new pieces of information those will also not be original ideas either. But yet, there is still a benefit to my own original curation of these pieces this information. My primary goal is for this [Digital Garden 🪴](Digital%20Garden%20%F0%9F%AA%B4.md) to be helpful for myself and a secondary goal is for this to be published and potentially helpful for someone else. While I don’t see anyone else using this site to the same extent that I am, it still might give someone a window into how I think and learn new topics. Who knows, maybe someone will enjoy stumbling upon [Rabbit Holes](../My%20Experiences/Rabbit%20Holes.md) that I also fell down and documented.
