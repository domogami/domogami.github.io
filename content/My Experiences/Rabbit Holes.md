## Why Rabbit Holes are Important to me

I derive a great deal of enjoyment and satisfaction from exploring a topic very deeply and learning the ins and outs of how things work. My favorite period of time in my life was the 6 months that I had after I graduated and before I started [Working at Amazon](Working%20at%20Amazon.md). During those 6 months I was free to create and learn but most importantly I was able to go down rabbit holes without needing to pull myself away. I was able to go as deep into a topic as I wanted because I had nothing that I really needed to do. Life felt free because I had a job lined up and I had finished my classes, I didn't need to interview and I didn't need to work. I was free to explore and fall down rabbit holes. I completely transformed the way I use [MacOS](../Software/MacOS.md) and I installed Asahi Linux on my M1 Max MacBook Pro.

I worked on rewriting my personal website using Remix and using AWS as a backend for a blog. I learned so much and you can read my [First Blog Post](../Blog%20Posts/First%20Blog%20Post.md) that was written before I decided that a [Digital Garden 🪴](../Concepts/Digital%20Garden%20%F0%9F%AA%B4.md) was a much better way to share my ideas. 

Going down rabbit holes is one of the things that I cherish most and I love learning new things about topics that I had no concept of and finding connections between seemingly unrelated ideas. I strive to be a lifelong learner and it sucks that [Working at Amazon](Working%20at%20Amazon.md) has prevented me from going down as many rabbit holes. Because of deadlines and sprint goals, I find myself needing to [Timebox](../Concepts/Timebox.md) and actually get real work done which is quite unfortunate but recently, I am making an effort to find more time to let myself fall down more rabbit holes once again.
