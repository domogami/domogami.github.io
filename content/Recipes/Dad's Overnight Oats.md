## Overnight Oats (Half)

- 6oz Oats
- 1.1oz Pecans/Almonds
- 1.5oz Golden Raisins
- 13oz Oat Milk or 2%
- 1oz Orange Juice
- 6oz Plain Yogurt
- 1oz Honey/granulated sugar
- 1/2 Banana
- .2 pb2
