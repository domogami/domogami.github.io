---
title: Mom’s Buttermilk Pancakes
---

## Wet

In a medium bowl, mix:

* 1 cup flour
* 1 tbsp sugar
* 1/2 tsp salt
* 1 tsp baking soda

## Dry

In a measuring cup, combine:

* 1 1/2 cup buttermilk (less makes fluffier)
* 1 egg
* 2 tbsp melted butter, cooled

## Combine

Add egg mixture to flour and gently fold together

‼️DO NOT OVER MIX‼️
