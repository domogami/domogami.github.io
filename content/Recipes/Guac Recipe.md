## Ingredients

- 1 Serrano Pepper
- 1 Garlic Clove
- 1 tsp of Kosher Salt
- 1 tsp Pepper (coarse)
- 2 tbsp Cilanto
- 1/2 tsp Garlic Powder
- 1 Lime
- 1/2 Cup Tomato (non-chilled and remove tomato white)
- 1/2 Cup Red Onion
- [Calidad Tortilla Chips](https://calidadchips.com/product/mexican-restaurant-style-corn-tortilla-chips/) or Restaurant Style or Blue Corn
